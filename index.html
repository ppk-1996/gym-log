3 days a week · full body · about 60 min

Three-Day Family Lift

One plan for all three of you. Everyone does the same exercises in the same order; only the weights change. Pick your name, then tap + Log weight after each exercise so next week you know where to start.

Suggested week (any 3 days with a rest day between)

How it works

Picking the weight

  • The "start" loads are first-day guesses. Pin stacks differ a lot between brands, so treat week 1 as calibration.
  • Right weight: the last 2 reps are hard, but your form stays clean and you could do 1–2 more.
  • Once you log a weight, the plan shows your last weight instead of the guess.

Getting stronger

  • Each exercise has a rep target, like 3×10. When you hit it on every set, go up next time: +5 lb on upper body, +10 lb on legs (one pin, or one 5 lb plate per side on the sled).
  • Assisted chin/dip is reversed: the pin is help. Lower it by 5–10 lb to progress. The progress table counts a lower assist as a gain.
  • Log reps too when you miss the target (e.g. "10, 9, 8") so you know to repeat the weight.

Training as a trio

  • Share each station and take turns: one lifts while two rest. That gives everyone about 90 seconds of rest without a timer.
  • Go lightest to heaviest (110 → 130 → 210) so you only add weight between turns.
  • One phone can log for everyone: switch the name at the top between turns.

After 8 weeks

  • If form is solid, swap goblet squat for a barbell back squat and DB Romanian deadlift for a barbell version.
  • Or add a 4th day of 30–40 min cardio.
  • Anyone with joint pain, back issues or a heart condition should check with a doctor first, and skip anything that hurts (soreness is fine, sharp pain is not).

Logged weights are saved to the family Google Sheet, so all three of you see the same numbers on any phone. The set circles are only a checklist on this phone. Sled leg press is logged as plates per side; dumbbells as each hand.
